What is EU PONKALA?

EU PONKALA is a real-time student accommodation marketplace that connects Eden University students directly with trusted landlords and boarding-house owners in Lusaka, Zambia.

Students browse verified properties with photos, prices, facilities, and live availability. Landlords list and manage their properties, update rooms, and receive inquiries. Admins oversee the entire ecosystem to keep it accurate, safe, and trustworthy.

Three roles, one platform

  • Students — search, filter, save, contact landlords, and get GPS directions. Free.
  • Landlords — list properties, manage rooms, update availability, and view analytics. Subscription-based.
  • Admins — verify properties, manage users, subscriptions, reports, and platform settings.

Core principles

  • Trust first — verified properties, accurate prices, real photos, real locations.
  • Live availability updated by landlords, with timestamps and audit logs.
  • Multi-channel contact — call, chat, WhatsApp, and GPS directions.
  • Never make a student dependent on the landlord being online.
