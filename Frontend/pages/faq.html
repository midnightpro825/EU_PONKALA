Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what students, landlords, and visitors ask most.

Is EU PONKALA free for students?
Yes. Students use EU PONKALA completely free — searching, saving favourites, contacting landlords, and getting directions.
How much does it cost for landlords?
The Standard plan is K300 for 3 months. A Premium plan (K500 / 3 months) adds featured listings, advanced analytics, and promotional placement. Plans are configurable by admins.
How do I know a property is legitimate?
Look for the EU PONKALA VERIFIED badge. Our team checks that the property exists, the location is confirmed, the landlord is verified, and photos, prices, and facilities are accurate.
What does "live availability" mean?
Landlords update room availability in real time. Every property shows a "last updated" timestamp so you know how fresh the data is.
What if the landlord is offline?
You're never blocked. You can still call, leave a message, use WhatsApp, and get GPS directions to visit the property directly.
How do I contact a landlord?
From any property page, tap Call, Message, or WhatsApp. You can also tap Get Directions to navigate there in person.
How do I report a fake or misleading listing?
Open the property, tap Report Issue, and choose the reason (false availability, wrong location, misleading photos, etc.). Our admins review every report.
Can I use EU PONKALA outside Eden University?
Not yet — we're starting with Eden University in Lusaka. Our architecture supports multiple universities and cities for future expansion.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept mobile money and card payments through trusted Zambian payment providers. All payments are confirmed server-side.
