Help Center

Step-by-step guides for students, landlords, and administrators.

👩‍🎓 For Students

Creating your account

Go to the Register page, choose "Student", fill in your name, email, phone, and password, then pick your university. You'll be logged in immediately.

Finding accommodation

Use the search bar or filters (price, distance, room type, facilities) to narrow down properties. Tap any property card to see full details, photos, rooms, and contact options.

Contacting a landlord

From any property page, tap Call, Message, or WhatsApp. You can also tap Get Directions to open GPS navigation.

Saving favourites

Tap the heart icon on any property. You'll get a notification when a saved property becomes available.

🏠 For Landlords

Adding your first property

Log in, open the sidebar, tap Add Property, and fill in the name, description, address, area, and monthly price. Upload photos and submit for verification.

Managing rooms

Open Rooms & Spaces to add rooms, set capacity, and update availability. Each update is timestamped and audited.

Responding to inquiries

Open Inquiries to view student messages. Reply by chat, call, or WhatsApp.

Subscription

Your listings stay active while your subscription is valid. Renew before expiry to avoid deactivation.

🛡️ For Admins

Verifying properties

Open Verification to review pending submissions. Approve or reject with notes. All actions are logged.

Managing users

View, suspend, or restore students and landlords from their respective sections.

Reports

Open Reports to review flagged properties or landlords and take action.
