Report an Issue

Let us know about inaccurate listings, safety concerns, or technical problems.

What can you report?

  • False availability — listing shows available but is actually full.
  • Incorrect price — displayed price differs from what the landlord charges.
  • Fake property — property doesn't exist or is not for rent.
  • Wrong location — GPS coordinates or address are incorrect.
  • Misleading photos — photos don't match the actual property.
  • Suspicious landlord — suspicious behavior or scam attempts.
  • Safety concern — anything unsafe about the property or area.
  • Other — anything else you think we should know.

Every report is reviewed by our admin team. Serious reports may result in the property being suspended or the landlord being investigated.
