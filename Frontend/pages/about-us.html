Our Story

About EU PONKALA

We're on a mission to make student accommodation simple, safe, and trustworthy.

Who we are

EU PONKALA is a Zambian-built technology platform created to solve a real problem: finding reliable student accommodation near university is stressful, time-consuming, and often full of outdated or misleading listings.

We started with Eden University in Lusaka and built a platform where students can search verified properties with real photos, live availability, and direct contact to landlords — and where landlords can manage their listings from one simple dashboard.

Our mission

To connect every student with safe, affordable, and verified accommodation — and to give every landlord the tools to manage vacancies with ease.

Our values

  • Trust first. Verified properties, accurate prices, real photos, real locations.
  • Student-focused. Free for students. Always.
  • Real-time. Live availability with timestamps and audit trails.
  • Community. Building a marketplace that helps landlords and students succeed together.

Where we're heading

Today: Eden University. Tomorrow: every university in Zambia, then beyond. Our architecture is multi-university from day one.
