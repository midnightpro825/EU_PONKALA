Privacy Policy

How EU PONKALA collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account information: name, phone, email, password (hashed), university or business name.
  • Landlord listings: property details, photos, prices, availability, GPS coordinates.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, searches performed, properties saved, inquiries sent.
  • Device & connection: IP address, browser type, and general location (city-level only).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate your account and connect students with landlords.
  • To show relevant properties, send notifications, and improve the Platform.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not expose student contact details publicly.
  • We do not continuously track student location without explicit permission.

4. Sharing

Landlords only receive information necessary to respond to their inquiries. Admins have access to platform data to enforce rules and maintain safety. Trusted service providers (e.g., payment processors, hosting) may process data on our behalf under strict agreements.

5. Data Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, role-based access, and audit logging. No system is 100% secure; please use a strong password and keep it private.

6. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Contact support@euponkala.com to exercise any of these rights.

7. Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal obligations. Deleted accounts are removed from active systems within 30 days.

8. Children

EU PONKALA is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced on the Platform.
